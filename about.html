About the project

Why we built this

"stocks4cas" exists to raise awareness of stock prediction — what it can do, what it can't, and why you should think twice before trusting anyone's crystal ball.

The mission

Demystify quantitative finance and algorithmic trading for students and curious learners. Knowledge is the best defence against hype.

The method

We simulate a realistic market with seeded randomness, then run six named strategies through a six-month backtest. Same data, very different outcomes.

The honesty

Everything here is simulated and educational. Real markets are noisy, unpredictable and unforgiving. We never claim otherwise.

Methodology & data

How the simulation was generated

Window: 1 March 2026 → 1 September 2026 (132 trading days).

Capital: Each strategy begins with $1,000,000 of simulated cash.

Prices: Geometric Brownian motion with per-ticker drift and volatility, seeded for reproducibility.

Metrics: Net P&L, total return, annualised Sharpe ratio and maximum drawdown.

Engine: Python + NumPy/Pandas (simulate.py), output as JSON consumed by this site.
